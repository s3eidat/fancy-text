Fancy Text

Type once, copy it in 28 styles for your bio, posts or name. These are Unicode characters, not fonts, so they paste anywhere that shows Unicode.

    Before you use it

    Screen readers may read these characters one by one (“mathematical bold capital S…”) or skip them, and search engines may not match them to normal words. Use them for decoration, not for names, links or anything people need to find or read. Only English letters and digits change; Arabic and other scripts stay as they are.
